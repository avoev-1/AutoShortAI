# ZIP05 Safety and Compliance Boundaries

ZIP05 supports legitimate multi-platform distribution and device administration.

Allowed:

- publishing owned or authorized content
- managing authorized platform accounts
- scheduling posts
- adapting metadata
- collecting analytics from authorized APIs
- administering devices owned or authorized by the user
- deploying files and tasks to managed devices
- monitoring health of racks, phones, PCs, servers and VMs

Not allowed in project scope:

- fake engagement
- artificial views, likes, comments, shares or follows
- botting platform interactions
- bypassing rate limits
- evading bans
- account farming for deceptive behavior
- credential theft or unauthorized account access
- bypassing platform security controls

Design rule:

Device Fleet Manager is an administration layer, not an engagement manipulation layer.
