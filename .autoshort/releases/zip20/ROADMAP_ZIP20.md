## Release Governance Rules

- Guardian check before implementation.
- VERIFY check before commit.
- No secrets committed.
- No platform lock-in.
- No SaaS drift.
- No mandatory heavy dependency drift.
- No restricted workflow enabled by default.
- No contract-breaking changes without migration.
